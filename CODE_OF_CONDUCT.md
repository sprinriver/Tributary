# Code of conduct

Tributary follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at **technologysprinriver@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban participants who break this code,
following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
